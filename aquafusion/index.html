What Is the AquaFusion Hydrogen Bottle?

The AquaFusion is a portable water ionizer that uses Solid Polymer Electrolyte (SPE) and Proton Exchange Membrane (PEM) technology to infuse molecular hydrogen (H₂) into drinking water. Unlike standard filtered water, hydrogen-rich water acts as a selective antioxidant, specifically targeting the most harmful free radicals in the human body.

What sets AquaFusion apart is its build quality. While competitors often use polycarbonate plastic that can leach chemicals, AquaFusion utilizes durable glass and stainless steel, ensuring that the water remains as pure as the technology intended.

How Does It Work?

The process is remarkably simple but scientifically complex. When you press the power button, an electric current passes through the water between two electrodes. The PEM membrane separates the hydrogen from the oxygen. The oxygen, chlorine, and ozone are vented out, while the pure molecular hydrogen is forced into the water under pressure.

Within 3 to 5 minutes, you will see a cloud of micro-bubbles — millions of hydrogen molecules ready to be ingested. Because molecular hydrogen is the smallest molecule in the universe, it can easily cross cell membranes and even the blood-brain barrier to provide cellular-level protection.
