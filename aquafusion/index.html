What is Hydrogen Water?

Hydrogen water is simply water with extra molecular hydrogen (H2) gas dissolved into it, acting as a powerful selective antioxidant.

In the world of bio-hacking and advanced wellness, molecular hydrogen has emerged as a key player. Unlike regular water, hydrogen-enriched water contains molecules that are small enough to penetrate cellular membranes, reaching the mitochondria and nucleus.

The **AquaFusion Hydrogen Bottle** uses advanced Solid Polymer Electrolyte (SPE) and Proton Exchange Membrane (PEM) technology. This process splits water molecules to infuse the water with pure H2 while venting out unwanted gases like chlorine and ozone.
